% Test of noteoverflow=foot (didactic) in the situations of marginfix's footcheck
% (the margin-foot branch): footnotes that don't fit in the margin go to the foot
% of their page, beside floats, \clearpage, ordinary footnotes and deferrable
% notes, in two columns, multicol, without room at the foot.  Each footnote made
% with \fn has a label and a saved position at its mark (m-N) and in its note
% (n-N); test-notefoot.py reads from them on which page and in which column each
% note is set, and the outcome from the PDF and the warnings, never a log line of
% the mechanism's.  The cases, by \def\testcase{...}:
%   dense     many long notes, more than the margins hold (24);
%   article   the same under article;
%   twocol    the same in two columns, where nothing goes to the foot;
%   multi     the same in multicols;
%   hyper     the same with hyperref;
%   counter   the same, with a counter stepped and labelled in each note;
%   nofeet    the same with \dimen\footins=0pt: no foot to go to;
%   drift     long notes on lines that can't break, then a blank of
%             \testdriftskip lines: no note lands away from its mark;
%   lastline  notes called out on the last lines of a page;
%   botfloat, topfloat, clear   with floats at the bottom or top, or \clearpage;
%   deferr    deferrable notes (\marginnotedeferrable) beside the footnotes,
%             with \marginnotepriority{\testprio};
%   order     long notes and a short one among them: at the foot in the order of
%             their marks;
%   minroom   a footnote that would leave less than 1.5 lines of text: not moved
%             to the foot, where the page would hold only floats;
%   fnrule    an ordinary footnote at the foot and a margin footnote going there:
%             one rule above them;
%   fnedge    a footnote that misses the foot by less than a point;
%   tall      long footnotes, some on one line (test-notefoot-tall.tex);
%   gen       generated: long notes, tall lines, floats, headings
%             (test-notefoot-dense.tex, labels only);
%   ownout    a document with an output routine of its own;
%   twotall   two margin notes, each taller than the room left beside its line,
%             and a footnote after them (they never settled under XeLaTeX in the
%             fork, review B-1): settled, each placed or warned about.
% With \testN set, the cases of test-notefoot-reins.tex instead (reins, reinsmix,
% wait, mcol3x, mcol3, mcol3n, waitsec, waitsh, rest2, local, defercarry,
% warnclaim, queue, cant), on letter paper.
