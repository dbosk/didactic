% Test of listings in multicols (didactic): its columns are set later, from what
% it has collected, so what keeps listings together can't know their height.
% The listings are left alone, and each one says so, with its line, once: a listing in
% a box (MP, a minipage, and MQ with a pair) has nothing to say, and a pair is
% one warning, whatever is in its cells (PL).  The same in multicols* (S).  A listing
% taller than \textheight (TL) says nothing about that, which isn't the limit here.
%
% Build: see the Makefile; test-check.py checks that there is no Overfull \vbox,
% that the lead-in stays near its listing (not stretched away from it), and that
% each listing and pair warns.
\documentclass[a4paper,oneside]{memoir}
\usepackage[british]{babel}
\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb,amsthm}
\usepackage{didactic}
\usepackage[noamsthm,notheorems]{beamerarticle}
\usepackage{multicol}
\makeatletter
\newwrite\@mcol@out
\newcount\@mcol@i
\newcommand\testlisting[1]{%
  \immediate\openout\@mcol@out=\jobname-#1.txt
  \@mcol@i\z@
  \@whilenum\@mcol@i<20\do{\advance\@mcol@i\@ne
    \immediate\write\@mcol@out{#1 \the\@mcol@i}}%
  \immediate\closeout\@mcol@out
  \VerbatimInput{\jobname-#1.txt}}
\newcommand\case[1]{\typeout{multicol case #1 line \the\inputlineno}}
\makeatother
